OBSERVEDESCRIBEMEASURE

To our knowledge, the first unified physical-caption benchmark spanning all three domains: third-person scenes, egocentric manipulation, and handheld-gripper demonstrations.

Videos contain contact, motion, deformation, and recovery. A caption can name the activity and still leave these processes out. What physical information survives when video becomes language?

Born from world-model pretraining practice, PhysCapBench provides a common yardstick: human-verified physical references, seven evaluation dimensions, and downstream tests in generation and manipulation.

Multiple camera views count as one episode.

02 / BUILDING THE YARDSTICK

What is a physical event?

A time-localized interaction: who acts on what, how contact or motion changes, and what state remains afterward.

01

Find the evidence

Inspect RGB frames. Hand motion, interaction cues, synchronized views, and gripper trajectories help identify changes worth revisiting.

02

Structure the process

Assign entity identities and roles. Record event intervals, motion, contact transitions, before-and-after states, and supported relations.

03

Verify every claim

Revisit the source frames, correct unsupported details, and check identities, timing, and coverage. Seven professional annotators verify and cross-check the records.

01 / WHAT THE YARDSTICK REVEALS

Better ordering can still lose the outcome.

Compressing EgoVid Dense into EgoVid Action raises temporal-order scores from 63.75 to 67.50, while states and outcomes fall from 70.88 to 60.62. A single overall score would hide that trade-off.

Third-person

Physis leads overall at 74.07 and tops five dimensions. LingBot Temporal leads temporal order (72.84), while Cosmos3 Narrative leads mechanisms and causality (71.00).

Egocentric

Physis leads overall at 79.36. Cosmos Ego leads contact and interaction (83.57) and temporal order (77.50). Different strategies retain different physical information.

02 / VIDEO RECONSTRUCTION

A missing recovery changes what gets generated.

The source shows soft toys compressed under a board, then recovering their volume. The physical caption includes recovery after unloading; the metadata caption ends with revealing deformed toys.

METADATA CAPTION · EXCERPT“then lifts the board to reveal the deformed toys”

Watch the state after each board is lifted: the physical-caption result retains volumetric toy shapes; Meta leaves flattened, sheet-like forms. The recovery speed and some later interactions remain imperfect. This is a selected qualitative example, not a measure of overall physical correctness.
